Purchase Invoices: A table to log vendor bills. Include a '3-Way Match' visual indicator (matching PO vs. GRN vs. Invoice Amount) before marking as 'Approved for Payment'.

Purchase Returns (Debit Notes): A simple UI to log items sent back to the vendor, including fields for Returned Qty, Reason (Damage/Wrong Item), and the linked PO/GRN number.
