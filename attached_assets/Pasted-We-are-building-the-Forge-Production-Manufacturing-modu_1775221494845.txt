We are building the 'Forge' (Production/Manufacturing) module for our ERP. This module must be universally adaptable to any industry (heavy manufacturing, apparel, food processing, manual assembly). Do not add any extra sub-modules.

1. Navigation (Sidebar Structure):
Create a new top-level parent menu called 'Forge'. It must contain EXACTLY these 6 sub-modules in this exact order:

Production Dashboard

Bill of Materials (BOM)

Workstations & Routing

Work Orders

Quality Control

Downtime Logs

2. Core Database Logic (Universal Adaptability):

Workstations: Must act as abstract 'Production Centers'. Fields: Name, Type (Enum: Machine, Manual Line, Vendor, QC Desk), Cost_Per_Hour, and Status (Active, Idle, Maintenance).

BOM: Must include two connected tables: BOM_Materials (what raw items are consumed) and BOM_Routing (which Workstations the product passes through, and estimated time at each).

Work_Orders: Fields for Product, Target_Qty, Produced_Qty, Status (Draft, In Progress, QC, Completed), and Assigned_Workstation.

3. Sub-module UI Requirements:

Production Dashboard: Top metric cards for Active Work Orders, Today's Yield, Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), and Scrap Rate. Include a Gantt chart or timeline view of active Work Orders across different Workstations.

Bill of Materials (BOM): A complex builder UI. Split into two sections: 'Material Requirements' (pulling from the Vault/Inventory catalog) and 'Operational Routing' (defining the sequence of Workstations).

Workstations & Routing: A resource management board. Show a grid of cards representing every active workstation, displaying its current status, the Work Order currently assigned to it, and its utilization rate. Include an 'Add Workstation' modal.

Work Orders: A Kanban board or a robust data table tracking production jobs. Moving a Work Order to 'Completed' must trigger a prompt to log 'Finished Good Yield' and 'Scrap/Waste Qty'.

Quality Control: An inspection ledger. Connects to completed Work Orders. Columns: Work Order ID, Inspected Qty, Passed Qty, Rejected Qty, and Rejection Reason.

Downtime Logs: A simple table to track inefficiencies. Fields: Workstation, Downtime Reason (Enum: Mechanical Failure, Material Shortage, Labor Absence), Start Time, End Time, and Total Minutes Lost.

Please implement this exact 6-part production architecture with a clean, industrial, and highly adaptable layout!
