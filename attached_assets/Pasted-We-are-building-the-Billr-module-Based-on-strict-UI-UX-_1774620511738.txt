We are building the 'Billr' module. Based on strict UI/UX designs, this is a localized, compliant financial engine separated into 4 sub-modules: Proformas, Tax Invoices, Receipts, and Credit Notes.

1. Database Schema (Invoices, Invoice_Items, Receipts):

Invoices: id, client_id, type (Enum: Proforma, Tax, Credit), document_number, po_reference, issue_date, due_date, subtotal, discount_amount, sgst_total, cgst_total, grand_total, notes, terms, status. (For Credit Notes, add reason_for_credit and invoice_reference).

Invoice_Items: id, invoice_id, description, hsn_sac, qty, unit (e.g., NOS, HRS), rate, tax_percentage, tax_amount, line_total.

Receipts: id, client_id, payment_date, payment_number, amount_received, bank_charges, payment_mode, deposit_to, reference, tax_deducted (boolean).

2. Main Billr UI (The 4 Tabs):

Create a top navigation with: 'Tax Invoices', 'Proformas', 'Receipts', and 'Credit Notes'.

Each tab shows a data table of its respective records. Columns: Date, Document#, Customer, Status (color-coded pills), Amount, Balance Due.

3. The Unified Document Builder (Full Screen):

Build a reusable Create/Edit screen for Invoices, Proformas, and Credit Notes.

Header Section: Split into two columns. Left: 'Billed By' (static company info) and 'Billed To' (Client dropdown). Right: Document Number, Date, Due Date, and PO Number. (If Credit Note: swap PO Number for 'Invoice Reference' and 'Reason' dropdown).

Line Items Table: Columns MUST exactly be: Items, HSN / SAC, Qty, Unit, Rate, Tax %, Tax Amt, Amount. Add an 'Add New Row' button.

Calculations & Summary: Bottom right corner. Must dynamically calculate and display: Sub Total, Discount (input field for flat amount), SGST (calculated), CGST (calculated), and Grand Total.

Footer: Textareas for 'Note & Terms'. Buttons for Cancel, Save Draft, Save & Send.

4. Record Payment (Slide-out Drawer):

Build a right-side slide-out drawer triggered from the Receipts tab.

Fields: Customer (dropdown), Payment Received (number), Bank Charges (number), Payment Date, Payment Number, Payment Mode (dropdown), Deposit To (dropdown), Reference (text), and Tax Deducted (toggle/radio).
